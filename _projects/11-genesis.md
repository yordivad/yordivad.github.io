**Genesis** holds the whole family: the languages, the runtime, the intelligence, the services and the
applications (CRM, ERP, a service desk, an integration platform, Muse for marketing, logistics apps). Around them I
built the tools that make quality and security the default: **Tsadi** derives the tests from a system's model,
**Ayin** judges the code (0 warnings, security A, reliability A, coverage of at least 95 %), **Litush** refactors until
Ayin is satisfied, **Katharsis** drives every change from branch to tag, and **Arkhos** writes the infrastructure and
deploys the microservices, watched by **Helios**.
