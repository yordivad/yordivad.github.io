**Turing** builds software the way an architect does. A paragraph about a project becomes a backlog discovered level
by level (context, sub-system, epic, feature, use case, story), every item judged by the architectural canon:
TOGAF, the SEI's quality attributes, Domain-Driven Design, UML. Only what is accepted is designed (the microservices,
the domain model with its aggregates, the databases, the screens), and only what is designed is built. Language
models propose; logic decides.
