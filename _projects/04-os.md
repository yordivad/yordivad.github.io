I am building an **embedded operating system** in freestanding Aleph: a Minix-style microkernel on RISC-V, with every
driver, the file system and the shell as user-space servers that are restarted when they fail. It runs on a RISC-V
machine I also wrote (RV64 with paging, interrupts and virtio devices). Above the drivers, machines are designed to
join into **one operating system** on actors and the database, updated live, never by turning a machine off.
